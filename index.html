"Pride goeth before destruction, and an haughty spirit before a fall. Better it is to be of an humble spirit with the lowly, than to divide the spoil with the proud." — Proverbs 16:18–19 (KJV)

Who You Are (Companion-POV Agency): You enter Seoul as the Fellow-Traveler walking beside 24-year-old session guitarist Elias Cho. You never wear Elias as a skin or speak his lines; your choices govern your own humility, your Stands in prayer, and what you freely give when no camera is watching.

Courage & Strength

Courage (1d6+6): Added to your transparent 2d6 roll in every Stand round.
Strength (2d6+12): Your endurance. If it reaches 0, you never die—Grace returns you to the Last Well keeping every Scroll and Treasure.

Favor & Treasure

Favor (1d6+6): Test Favor (2d6 <= Favor) when you lose a Stand round to halve your Strength loss.
★ Treasure (Starts at 0): Rises ONLY when you choose to Give (+1) at personal cost instead of Keep (+0).

How to Play · The Narrow Way Walk

Zone I: Elias Cho & David the King · Companion-POV Guide
What is a Stronghold? A Stronghold is a spiritual lie or habit you stand against with God's Word.
1. Movement & Camera

Desktop: Walk with WASD or Arrow keys, drag to look, scroll to zoom (clamped cleanly behind you), and press E or click a node to interact.
Phone: Drag the bottom-left joystick to walk and tap nodes or chips to interact.

2. Stands, Dice & Victory Law

Before Round 1, apply Step 1: The Victory Law (humility/confession) and speak a resonant KJV Scroll to lower the Stronghold's Courage & Strength. Each round rolls transparent 2d6 + Courage vs the Stronghold's 2d6 + Courage. Higher total reduces the other side's Strength by 2.

3. Courage, Strength, Favor & Wells

Courage adds to every Stand roll. Strength is endurance. When you lose a round, you may Test Favor (2d6 <= Favor, costs 1 Favor) to halve the loss from -2 to -1. Wells of Living Water restore Favor to full, heal +4 Strength, and teach KJV Scrolls. If Strength hits 0, Grace returns you to the Last Well keeping all Scrolls & Treasure.

4. Oil, Bread, Scrolls & Treasure

Oil: Restores +4 Strength during a Stand.
Bread: Restores +2 Strength at a Well or can be shared with neighbors in need.
Scrolls: Verbatim KJV Scriptures learned at Wells.
★ Treasure: Rises ONLY when you freely choose to Give (+1) rather than Keep (+0).
