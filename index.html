Who You Are (Companion-POV Agency): You enter Seoul as the Fellow-Traveler walking beside 24-year-old session guitarist Elias Cho. You never wear Elias as a skin or speak his lines; your choices govern your own humility, your Stands in prayer, and what you freely give when no camera is watching.

Courage & Strength (Watchman Balance)

Courage (9): Added to your transparent 2d6 roll in every Stand round against Strongholds (2d6 + Roll Bonus).
Strength (20 max): Your endurance (ties cost 1 Strength; lost rounds cost 2). If Strength reaches 0, "Fallen, but not forsaken" (Micah 7:8 KJV) restores Strength to 50% (10/20), deducts 1 Treasure if you have any, and sends you to the start of the current stage.

Favor, Wells & Sacrificial Treasure

Favor (9 max) & Wells: Test Favor (2d6 <= Favor, costs 1 Favor) to halve a lost round, or use Oil (+4) & Bread (+2) mid-fight (plus 1 extra Bread pickup near Well 3 in Stage IV). Each Well heals +3 Strength & +1 Favor once.
★ Treasure (Starts at 0): Each Give (+1 Treasure) costs 2 Strength OR 1 Favor (4+ Treasure reaches the Section 300 True Ending).

How to Play · The Narrow Way Walk

Zone I: Elias Cho & David the King · Watchman Balance Guide
Watchman Difficulty — Stand Firm: A Stronghold is a spiritual lie or habit you stand against with God's Word.
1. Movement & Camera

Desktop: Walk with WASD or Arrow keys, drag to look, scroll to zoom (clamped cleanly behind you), and press E or click a node to interact.
Phone: Drag the bottom-left joystick to walk and tap nodes or chips to interact.

2. Stands, Ties & Victory Law

Before Round 1, apply Step 1: The Victory Law and speak a resonant KJV Scroll to lower the Stronghold's roll bonus & Strength. Each round rolls 2d6 + Courage vs the Stronghold's 2d6 + Roll Bonus (e.g. 2d6+9). Wins deal 2 damage to the Stronghold; losses cost 2 Strength; ties cost 1 Strength. Walking away on a Victory Law walk-out path costs 1 Favor or 1 Strength.

3. Wells & Cost of Failure

Each Well of Living Water can be used once, restoring +3 Strength and +1 Favor and teaching a KJV Scroll. When you lose a round, you may Test Favor (2d6 <= Favor, costs 1 Favor) to halve the loss. If Strength reaches 0, "Fallen, but not forsaken" (Micah 7:8 KJV) restores Strength to 50% (10/20), loses 1 Treasure (if any), and sends you to the start of the current stage with that Stand un-cleared.

4. Oil, Bread & Sacrificial Treasure

Oil (+4 Strength) and Bread (+2 Strength) are your main ways to recover mid-fight inside a Stand (Bread can also be eaten at a Well, with 1 extra Bread pickup near Well 3 in Stage IV).
★ Treasure: Each Give costs a real sacrifice—2 Strength OR 1 Favor (your choice)—for +1 Treasure. Reach 4+ Treasure with all Stands cleared for Section 300 (True Ending).
